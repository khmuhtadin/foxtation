## What's new

- **Main window.** Home, History, Insights, Dictionary, Snippets, Style and Settings in one window, opened from the menu bar fox (or by opening the app again).
  - **Home** shows today's dictations and stats. **History** lists every dictation with search and copy (and playback, if you keep recordings). **Insights** shows speaking speed, time saved, cloud fixes, apps and a streak.
  - **Dictionary**: names and terms to spell right, plus replacements, optionally per app. Suggestions come from your history.
  - **Snippets**: say a cue, get a saved block of text.
  - **Style**: a cleanup style per kind of app, and spoken emoji.
  - History stays on your Mac; **Settings › Data & Privacy** sets how long, and exports or deletes it.
- **WhisperKit engine, now the default.** Whisper large-v3 turbo on the Neural Engine. It transcribes while you speak, so the text is ready about 0.5 s after you let go (was about 3 s). MLX and whisper.cpp are still available in **Settings › Engine**.
- **Foxtation Pro.** Optional paid cloud cleanup: removes filler words, applies self-corrections, fixes punctuation, uses your dictionary and the style you picked for the app. Only text is sent, never audio. Dictations with nothing to fix are pasted right away without the cloud (**Skip when there's nothing to fix**, on by default). If the cloud can't be reached, the raw transcript is pasted. [Get a license](https://buy.polar.sh/polar_cl_pGAbwRd3v6HXPARELwKUmTJlleM9LQDteqWRq41Bn7C), then activate it in **Settings › License**.
- **Simpler pill.** It fades in at the bottom center of the screen, shows a timer while listening, and offers Undo after a cloud cleanup.

## Fixes

- ⌘V, ⌘C and ⌘A work in text fields.
- A ⌘-only dictation shortcut (such as ⌘V) is no longer accepted, because it took over pasting. A stored one is reset to ⌃⌥⌘D.

## Upgrading

- On first launch Foxtation opens **Settings › Engine**. Press **Download**: the model is 1.6 GB, and the first load prepares it for the Neural Engine, which takes about a minute once. The row shows the progress and says **Ready** when you can dictate.
- If you picked MLX or whisper.cpp yourself before, Foxtation keeps using it. Switch to WhisperKit in **Settings › Engine**.
- The default engine doesn't need `uv`. Only MLX does.
- Microphone and Accessibility permissions carry over: this release is signed with the same certificate as 0.1.2.
- The pill placement setting is gone; the pill is always at the bottom center.

## Known limitations

- Not notarized by Apple. After installing the DMG, allow the first launch in **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Foxtation.app`. The Homebrew cask does this for you.
- Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.
