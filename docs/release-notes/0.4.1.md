## What's new

- **A friendlier installer.** Opening the disk image now shows what to do: drag the fox into Applications, with an arrow from one to the other, in Foxtation's colors. The mounted disk carries the Foxtation icon.
- **Download straight from the website.** The Download buttons on foxtation.khaisa.studio now download the app directly instead of opening the Releases page.

Nothing else changed since 0.4.0.

## Upgrading

Install over 0.4.0. Your settings, license, speech model and permissions stay as they are.
