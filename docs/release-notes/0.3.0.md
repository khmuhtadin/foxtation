## What's new

Much more accurate dictation, in 23 languages, at the same speed.

- **Languages you speak.** Pick them in **Settings › General** (the first time, Foxtation starts from your Mac's languages plus English). Auto detect now only chooses among those, so a few seconds of Indonesian no longer come out as Tagalog or Korean, and Dutch no longer comes out as German. The menu bar's **Language** menu lists Auto and your languages.
- **Quiet voices are heard.** Foxtation used to treat anything below a fixed loudness as silence, so a quiet voice or a distant mic could lose whole sentences. It now measures your room's own noise and listens relative to it. Room noise no longer turns into made-up words like "Terima kasih." or "Thank you." either.
- **Whole phrases.** Speech is transcribed in longer stretches, cut at real pauses, so sentences no longer break in the middle ("ke email aku. Sebelum jam 5").
- **New languages.** Swedish, Danish, Norwegian and Finnish, for 23 in all.
- **Pro cleanup fixes misheard words.** English terms inside another language often come out spelled by sound; cleanup now fixes them from context ("batch kent cek kout" → "backend checkout", "preaching-nya" → "pricing-nya"), joins sentences that were split at a pause, and spaces Indonesian di/ke correctly ("dimana" → "di mana"). Every dictation longer than four words now goes through cleanup, because a misheard word can't be spotted on the Mac.

## Pricing

## Upgrading

Install over 0.2.x. Your settings, license, speech model and permissions stay as they are. Check **Settings › General › Languages you speak** once: add any language you dictate in that isn't listed.
