## What's new

A design pass over the whole app. Nothing about how dictation works has changed.

- **Clearer and easier to read.** Darker accent, green and pink so small text and white-on-color pass contrast. One type scale, tabular digits for times and counts, and nothing shifts when you select a tab or sidebar item.
- **Easier to hit.** Every button, switch, chip and the pill's × and Undo are at least 28 pt. Switches clearly read on or off, also when the window isn't in front.
- **Consistent controls.** Search fields, text fields and the shortcut recorder share one style. The Engine and Advanced settings now look like the rest of Settings. Buttons respond to hover and press.
- **History.** Right-click a dictation to copy it, copy what Whisper heard, or delete it. Long text shows in full on hover.
- **Pill.** A crisp state border instead of a glow.
- **Dark mode.** The Snippets intro and the License header no longer glow white.
- **VoiceOver.** Switches, tabs and the range picker announce their state.

## Upgrading

Install over 0.2.0. Your settings, license, speech model and permissions stay as they are.
