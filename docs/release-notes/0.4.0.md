## What's new

- **Foxtation Pro: nothing to download.** Activate your license and dictate right away. Pro now transcribes in Foxtation Cloud while you talk: ElevenLabs Scribe v2 for the first hour each day, then Whisper large-v3, up to 3 hours a day. No 1.6 GB model, no minute of setup.
- **More accurate with Pro.** Across our 23-language test set, Scribe made about half the errors of the on-device model (4.9% of words vs 10.7%). On casual Indonesian mixed with English it got "pricing-nya" and "timeline-nya" where the on-device model wrote "preaching-nya" and "timelineenya".
- **Works offline too.** If the on-device model is installed, it takes over with the same recording when there's no connection. With Cloud selected, **Settings › Engine** shows it as an optional download.
- **Your choice.** Activating switches **Settings › Engine** to **Cloud**. Pick WhisperKit there to keep your voice on the Mac; the free version stays fully on-device.
- **English style names.** Casual, Neutral, Polished and Literal, with English examples.

## Speed

Text appears about 0.3–0.7 s after you let go with Cloud (0.2–0.5 s on-device), plus about 1 s when cleanup runs.

## Privacy

With Cloud, each stretch of speech goes through the Foxtation server to ElevenLabs or Groq to be transcribed. Foxtation stores no audio or text. **Settings › Data & Privacy** says which engine you're on.

## Upgrading

Install over 0.3.x. Your settings, license and permissions stay as they are. Pro users switch to Cloud the next time they activate; to switch now, pick **Cloud** in **Settings › Engine**.
